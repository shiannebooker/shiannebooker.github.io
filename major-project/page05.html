If you are unsure what items should be essential or non-essential, I have made an itemized list to help you.

Essential Items

  • Grocieries
  • Gas (If you drive)
  • Any and all bills
  • Self-care products that you buy on a regular basis
  • Household items you buy on a regular basis

Non-Essential Items

  • Clothes
  • Fast Food
  • Decor
  • Ultimatly anything that does not serve a purpose in your everyday life, and you don't absolutely need it.

This being said, I don't mean that you should never buy non-essential items, I just mean to limit how much you are buying them.
