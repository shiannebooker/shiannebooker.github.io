About The Creator

I am a young adult just like you who was constantly trying to make ends meet, and more often than not, unsucessful. I tried the newest budgeting apps like RocketMoney, tried Dave Ramsey's scheme. But what I realized the most is that the older generations do not understand what it is like to be broke in todays day & age. 20 dollars is what 1 dollar was 50 years ago. Gas was less than a dollar 70 years ago. It's just ridiculous. So I founded Head Start Society. A website by young people, for young people. I hope that if you take away nothing at all from this website, you can at least relate and know the struggle and know that it isnt easy to handle finances. But the notion of trying is enough.
