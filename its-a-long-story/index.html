Revenge of the Sith... In a nutshell.

The Clone wars are ending, and Anakin is seen as a hero. Chancelor Palpatine is slowly gaining control of the Republic, and only Anakin is catching on. The rest of the counsil thinks Anakin is not mature enough to be on the senate, and ignores his concerns about the chancelor.

Padme Amidala is working on the senate, trying to preserve what's left of the democracy, while it keeps slowly slipping away.
